Changes for SpineLab:
Selected 24 vertebrae, sacrum and 23 discs; welded duplicate vertices; packed indexed meshes into GLB; transformed axes from source (left, posterior, superior) into application (right, superior, posterior), centered and scaled uniformly by 0.04. Original neutral surface shapes retained. Smooth normals computed at runtime. Materials added. Modified disc scenarios deform source disc meshes; adjacent bones shift vertically to illustrate height reduction. Neural structures are separately constructed schematic overlays. These are educational illustrations, not validated biomechanical or patient-specific simulations.

The redistributed anatomical GLB and its deformed anatomical derivatives remain available under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Sources and SHA-256 checksums are in sources.json. No endorsement by DBCLS is implied.

Body overlay update: FMA7163 skin from the same converted BodyParts3D distribution. Vertex clustering at 8 mm (4 mm in the head) and duplicate/degenerate triangle removal reduce download and rendering cost. The identical original spine coordinate transform is applied; there is no independent body rescaling. body-source.json records its source and checksum. Articulated motion and skinning are SpineLab derivatives, unvalidated for clinical use. The body GLB and derivatives retain CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.

Body surface repair: external genital detail smoothed into a neutral pelvic envelope; closed outer skin reconstructed from anterior/posterior scan envelopes at 6 mm and Taubin-smoothed. This changes surface detail while retaining dataset registration and uniform scale.
